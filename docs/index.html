    Method

    What is being compared

    Three worked pairs, one per tab selection above:

    • SST-2 sentiment, fp16 vs int8 - two builds of the pinned checkpoint Xenova/distilbert-base-uncased-finetuned-sst-2-english at revision 0b6928ef, the canonical "ship the half-size artifact?" question (67 MB int8 vs 134 MB fp16), over the 872-sentence GLUE SST-2 validation split.
    • AG News topics, full vs pruned - a tf-idf + logistic regression classifier (100k features) against its 50%-pruned build (weakest half of the features zeroed, nothing refit), over a seeded 2,000-row sample of the public AG News test split. Four classes, so the per-class breakdown matters.
    • California housing, full vs shrunk - a histogram gradient boosting regressor (600 trees, 63 leaves) against a shrunk serving build (120 trees, 15 leaves), over a seeded 1,200-row sample of the 20% hold-out. Regression: the paired statistic runs on per-row absolute error.

    The statistics

    • Paired bootstrap (10,000 resamples, seed 1337): resample examples with replacement, recompute the delta each time, take the percentile interval. Pairing on the same examples is what makes a 1.5-point gap on 872 sentences resolvable.
    • McNemar's test, classification (exact two-sided binomial): only the discordant examples carry signal; 17 flips for the worse against 4 for the better on SST-2 gives p = 0.0072. The chi-square approximation agrees (p = 0.0088).
    • Sign test, regression (exact two-sided binomial): rows where the new build's absolute error grew against rows where it shrank; ties excluded. On the housing pair, 760 worse against 440 better gives p < 0.0001.
    • Cohen's h: effect size for two proportions - small in aggregate on SST-2 (-0.05), which is exactly why per-slice analysis matters.
    • Fisher exact enrichment: per slice, a one-sided hypergeometric test of whether regressions concentrate there more than the base rate predicts.

    The gate

    classification: ship ⇤ McNemar p ≥ alpha AND CI lower bound ≥ -maxDrop
    regression:    ship ⇤ sign test p ≥ alpha AND CI upper bound ≤ maxIncrease

    Defaults: maxDrop = 0.5 points, maxIncrease = 0.02 target units, alpha = 0.05. The gate is honest both ways - a significant regression holds the build, and an inconclusive comparison says so instead of guessing. Tune it on the Verdict tab.

    Gate it in CI

    The same engine ships as a zero-dependency CLI. Give it one combined CSV (or separate gold/v1/v2 files), and it prints the verdict, writes a markdown or JSON report, and exits 0 on ship, 1 on hold:

    node cli/backslide.mjs compare --combined preds.csv --max-drop 0.5 --md report.md

    A composite GitHub Action wraps it: uses: Aditya2375/backslide@v1 with your CSV paths as inputs posts the report into the job summary and fails the job on a hold. A working example runs in .github/workflows/gate-example.yml against the committed sample CSVs.

    Reproduce every number

    node scripts/measure-pair.mjs re-runs both pinned ONNX builds over SST-2; scripts/measure-agnews.py and scripts/measure-housing.py rebuild the other two pairs with scikit-learn; node scripts/compute-snapshot.mjs recomputes every statistic on this page from the committed golden sets. CI recomputes all snapshots and fails on any drift.

    Honest limits

    • Three pairs, three task shapes - still illustrative, not exhaustive. The method - paired gating on a golden set - is the point, not these particular models.
    • Slice tags are transparent heuristics, not human annotation; treat per-slice p-values as triage, not verdicts.
    • The AG News and housing pairs are measured offline with scikit-learn and committed as data; only the SST-2 pair also runs live in your browser. Browser wasm arithmetic can drift a last-decimal score from the Node pipeline; every reported number comes from the committed artifacts, not your browser.
    • Your-models uploads are parsed as text and never sent anywhere, but the analysis trusts your files: wrong columns in, wrong verdict out. The detect-automatically task guess is a hint - set the task explicitly when it matters.

    backslide runs entirely in your browser against pinned, committed artifacts. Source, golden data, CLI and reproduction scripts: github.com/Aditya2375/backslide.
